Frequently Asked Questions

Do you charge for your services?

No! My services are typically free to you. I'm compensated by resorts, cruise lines, and travel partners, so you get expert planning at no additional cost.

How far in advance should I book?

For best availability and pricing, I recommend booking 6-12 months in advance for peak travel times. However, I can often help with last-minute trips as well!

What if something goes wrong during my trip?

I provide 24/7 support for my clients. If any issues arise during your vacation, you can reach me directly and I'll work to resolve them immediately.

Can you help with group travel?

Absolutely! I specialize in group trips including destination weddings, family reunions, corporate retreats, and multi-family vacations.

Do I have to book all-inclusive?

Not at all! While I specialize in all-inclusive resorts, I can help with any type of vacation - from boutique hotels to luxury cruises to European tours.
