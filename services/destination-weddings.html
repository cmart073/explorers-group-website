Your Dream Destination Wedding Awaits

Imagine exchanging vows with your toes in the sand, surrounded by turquoise waters and your closest friends and family. Destination weddings at all-inclusive resorts combine your ceremony, reception, and honeymoon in one unforgettable celebration - often at a fraction of the cost of a traditional wedding.

As your destination wedding specialist, I'll handle every detail from resort selection to guest room blocks, wedding package coordination, and ensuring your big day is absolutely perfect.

How All-Inclusive Resort Weddings Work

Free Wedding Packages

Most all-inclusive resorts offer FREE wedding packages when you meet minimum room night requirements (typically 5-10 rooms for 3 nights). These packages include:

  • Wedding coordinator services
  • Ceremony location (beach, garden, or terrace)
  • Legal or symbolic ceremony officiant
  • Bridal bouquet and groom's boutonniere
  • Wedding cake (typically 2-3 tiers)
  • Sparkling wine toast
  • Reserved dinner at resort restaurant
  • Basic decor (chairs, aisle runner, altar setup)

Upgraded Wedding Packages

Want more? Enhanced packages ($2,000-10,000+) can include:

  • Private reception spaces
  • Upgraded menus and open bar
  • Live music or DJ
  • Professional photography and videography
  • Spa day for bride and bridesmaids
  • Upgraded floral arrangements
  • Fireworks or special lighting
  • Welcome cocktail party for guests

Legal vs. Symbolic Ceremonies

Legal Marriages

Get legally married at the resort:

  • Requires paperwork (varies by country)
  • Usually needs documents submitted 2-4 weeks in advance
  • May require blood tests or witness requirements
  • Marriage certificate issued at destination
  • Easiest countries: Jamaica, Mexico, Aruba, Turks & Caicos

Symbolic Ceremonies

Have a ceremony without legal paperwork:

  • Get legally married at home before or after
  • No waiting periods or documentation
  • More flexibility with ceremony content
  • Same beautiful experience, less stress
  • Popular choice for couples wanting full control

Guest Travel & Group Rates

I'll coordinate all guest travel and secure the best group rates:

  • Group Pricing: Discounted room rates for wedding blocks
  • Free Rooms: Often 1 free room per 10 booked (for bride/groom or close family)

Typical Guest Costs

Your guests will pay for their own travel. Typical 3-night costs per person (double occupancy):

  • Mexico/Dominican Republic: $800-2,000 per person (flight + resort)
  • Jamaica: $1,000-2,500 per person
  • Other Caribbean: $1,200-3,000 per person

Guests get a vacation + your wedding. Most find it a great value!

Frequently Asked Questions

How much does a destination wedding cost?

For the couple: Free wedding packages are available, or $2,000-10,000 for upgraded packages. Your own resort stay is typically $3,000-8,000 for the week. Much less than traditional weddings!

How many guests typically attend?

Most destination weddings have 30-75 guests. Smaller is fine - some couples have only 10-20 close family and friends.

Do we have to book our honeymoon separately?

No! Many couples simply extend their stay at the same resort. You can upgrade to a better room or change resorts after the wedding if you prefer.

What if guests can't afford to come?

It's normal - destination weddings tend to be smaller. Consider offering group payment plans, and remember that you'll have quality time with those who attend.
