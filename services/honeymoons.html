Honeymoon Budget Guide

Honeymoons can work for any budget. Here's what to expect:

  • Budget-Friendly ($3,000-5,000): Caribbean all-inclusive resorts, shorter trips (4-5 nights), standard rooms
  • Mid-Range ($5,000-8,000): Premium all-inclusive resorts, suite upgrades, 7 nights, some excursions
  • Luxury ($8,000-15,000): Five-star resorts, overwater bungalows, butler service, longer stays, premium locations
  • Ultra-Luxury ($15,000+): Private villas, exotic destinations (Maldives, Bora Bora), multi-destination trips

These are general estimates for two people and vary by destination, season, and length of stay.

Best Time to Book Your Honeymoon

I recommend booking your honeymoon 6-9 months before your wedding date for the best selection and pricing. However, I can often accommodate last-minute honeymoons as well!

Planning Timeline:

  • 9-12 months before: Book for peak season or exotic destinations
  • 6-9 months before: Ideal booking window for most destinations
  • 3-6 months before: Still good availability, some seasonal deals
  • Last minute: I can often find great deals for flexible couples
