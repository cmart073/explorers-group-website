Group Travel

Bring everyone together for unforgettable all-inclusive group vacations

Stress-Free Group Vacation Planning

Coordinating group travel can be overwhelming - different budgets, schedules, and preferences. All-inclusive resorts simplify everything by offering group rates, inclusive amenities, and something for everyone. Let me handle the logistics while you focus on making memories together.

Perfect For Group Vacations

  • Family Reunions: Multi-generational trips with activities for all ages
  • Friend Getaways: Milestone birthdays, girls' trips, guys' weekends
  • Destination Weddings: Ceremony + vacation for all guests
  • Corporate Retreats: Team building in paradise
  • Anniversary Celebrations: Bring family and friends to celebrate
  • Multi-Family Trips: Several families vacationing together
  • Sports Teams & Organizations: End-of-season celebrations
  • Bachelor/Bachelorette Parties: All-inclusive fun in the sun

Benefits of All-Inclusive for Groups

  • Group Rates: Discounted pricing for 5+ rooms
  • Free Rooms: Typically 1 free room per 10 paid (for organizers or honored guests)
  • No Bill Splitting: Everyone pays their own way upfront
  • Something for Everyone: Multiple restaurants, activities, and pool areas
  • Together & Apart: Spend time together then retreat to your own space
  • Easy Budgeting: Each person knows exact costs upfront
  • Group Perks: Exclusive amenities, cocktail parties, resort credits

How Group Bookings Work

Typical Group Requirements

  • Minimum 5-10 rooms to qualify for group rates
  • Must travel together (same dates)
  • Same resort and arrival/departure dates
  • Rooms can be different categories

Group Pricing Structure

  • Small Deposit: $100-300 per room to hold space
  • Name Changes Allowed: Flexible if someone cancels
  • Final Payment: 45-60 days before travel
  • Individual Payments: Each room pays separately - no group leader financial burden

Complimentary Room Ratio

Most resorts offer free rooms based on paid rooms:

  • 1 free room per 10 paid rooms (most common)
  • 1 free room per 7-8 paid rooms (premium promotions)
  • Free rooms can upgrade to better categories
  • Perfect for organizer, bride/groom, or honored guests

Group Travel Timeline

12-18 Months Before

  • Contact me with dates and group size
  • Select destination and resort
  • Secure group contract with deposit
  • Send save-the-dates to potential travelers

Typical Group Costs

Per person for 7 nights at all-inclusive resort:

  • Mexico (Cancun/Riviera Maya): $1,200-2,500 per person (flight + resort)
  • Jamaica: $1,400-2,800 per person
  • Dominican Republic: $1,100-2,200 per person
  • Aruba/Curaçao: $1,600-3,000 per person

Group rates typically save 10-25% off regular pricing. Prices include airfare from major US cities.

Payment Made Easy

  • Each guest pays individually - no group leader collecting payments
  • Flexible payment plans with deposits and installments
  • Online payment portals for convenience
  • Travel insurance available for peace of mind

Tips for Successful Group Trips

  • Book Early: Best availability and pricing 9-12 months out
  • Clear Communication: Set expectations about costs and schedule
  • Respect Independence: Plan some group activities but allow free time
  • Create a Group Chat: WhatsApp or Facebook group for easy coordination
  • Designate Point Person: One contact for me to coordinate with
  • Consider Excursions: Group tours often get discounts

Minimum Group Sizes

  • 5-9 Rooms: Group rates, usually no free rooms
  • 10-19 Rooms: 1 free room, enhanced perks
  • 20-29 Rooms: 2 free rooms, VIP treatment
  • 30+ Rooms: Multiple free rooms, private events, maximum perks
