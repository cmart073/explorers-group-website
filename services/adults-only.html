Adults-Only All-Inclusive Resorts

Sophisticated relaxation in romantic, child-free paradise

Experience Tranquil, Adults-Only Luxury

Adults-only all-inclusive resorts offer a sophisticated, child-free environment perfect for couples, honeymooners, and friends seeking relaxation, romance, and premium amenities. Enjoy gourmet dining, top-shelf drinks, quiet pools, and an atmosphere designed exclusively for grown-ups.

What Makes Adults-Only Resorts Special?

  • Peaceful Atmosphere: No kids means quieter pools, restaurants, and beaches
  • Romantic Ambiance: Couples-focused activities and intimate settings
  • Premium Everything: Elevated dining, top-shelf liquor, luxurious accommodations
  • Sophisticated Entertainment: Live music, wine tastings, cooking classes vs kids' shows
  • Swim-Up Bars: Adult beverage service right in the pool
  • Spa & Wellness: Tranquil spa experiences and adult fitness programs
  • Late-Night Vibes: Nightclubs, beach parties, and entertainment for grown-ups

Best Adults-Only Resort Brands

Sandals Resorts

The ultimate couples-only all-inclusive experience:

  • 18 Caribbean locations (Jamaica, St. Lucia, Bahamas, Barbados, Grenada, Antigua, Curaçao)
  • All-suite accommodations with many swim-up options
  • Up to 16 restaurants per resort (French, Italian, Asian, Caribbean)
  • Unlimited premium spirits - Robert Mondavi wines, Grey Goose vodka, Tanqueray gin
  • Scuba diving included (certified and lessons)
  • Butler service in select room categories
  • Water sports, fitness classes, golf (at some resorts)

Secrets Resorts & Spas

Unlimited-Luxury® for adults in Mexico and Caribbean:

  • Locations: Mexico (Riviera Maya, Cancun, Los Cabos, Puerto Vallarta), Jamaica, St. Lucia, Barbados, Dominican Republic
  • Preferred Club upgrades with private lounges and beach areas
  • 24-hour room service and concierge
  • Premium spirits and wine
  • Multiple pools including infinity and swim-up bars
  • World-class spas

Excellence Resorts

Adults-only elegance:

  • Excellence Playa Mujeres (Cancun)

Breathless Resorts & Spas

Hyatt Zilara

  • Swim-up infinity pools

Adults-Only vs. Couples-Only

Adults-Only (18+ or 21+)

  • Singles, couples, and friends welcome
  • Larger selection of resorts
  • Good for girlfriend getaways and guys' trips
  • More variety in atmosphere (relaxed to party)

Couples-Only

  • Must be traveling as a couple (married, engaged, or in a relationship)
  • Sandals and Couples Resorts only
  • More romantic, intimate vibe
  • Perfect for honeymoons and anniversaries
