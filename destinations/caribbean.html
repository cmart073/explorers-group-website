Discover the Caribbean Islands

The Caribbean offers some of the world's most beautiful vacation destinations, from the white-sand beaches of the Bahamas to the lush rainforests of St. Lucia. Whether you're planning a romantic honeymoon, family vacation, or luxury getaway, the Caribbean has the perfect island for you.

Popular Caribbean Destinations

Jamaica

All-inclusive resorts, vibrant culture, and stunning beaches in Montego Bay, Negril, and Ocho Rios

St. Lucia

Romantic honeymoon destination with iconic Pitons, luxury resorts, and volcanic beaches

Bahamas

Crystal-clear waters, swimming pigs, and world-class diving in Nassau and Paradise Island

Barbados

British charm meets Caribbean beauty with pink sand beaches and luxury accommodations

Antigua

365 beaches - one for every day of the year - with world-class sailing and resorts

Aruba

One Happy Island with sunny weather, beautiful beaches, and Dutch-Caribbean culture

Why Choose the Caribbean?

  • All-Inclusive Convenience: World-renowned all-inclusive resorts where everything is included - meals, drinks, activities, and entertainment
  • Easy to Reach: Short flights from major US cities with direct routes to most islands
  • Year-Round Sunshine: Perfect weather nearly every day with temperatures in the 80s
  • Diverse Experiences: From adventure activities to relaxing spa days, water sports to cultural exploration
  • Romance & Luxury: Ideal for honeymoons with adults-only resorts, overwater bungalows, and couples-only packages

When to Visit the Caribbean

The Caribbean is a year-round destination, but the best time to visit depends on your preferences:

  • Peak Season (December-April): Perfect weather, less rain, but higher prices
  • Shoulder Season (May & November): Great deals, good weather, fewer crowds
  • Summer (June-August): Hot and humid, but significant savings on resorts
  • Hurricane Season (August-October): Best deals, but weather can be unpredictable
