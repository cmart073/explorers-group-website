Jamaica All-Inclusive Resorts

Experience the rhythm of the Caribbean with world-class all-inclusive properties

Welcome to Jamaica - Land of All-Inclusive Excellence

Jamaica is the birthplace of all-inclusive resort vacations and remains the gold standard for hassle-free tropical getaways. With stunning beaches, vibrant culture, delicious cuisine, and some of the world's best all-inclusive resorts, Jamaica offers the perfect escape for honeymooners, families, and groups.

Top Jamaica Resort Areas

Montego Bay (MoBay)

Jamaica's second-largest city and tourism capital. Home to the largest concentration of all-inclusive resorts, Montego Bay offers:

  • Convenient airport access (Sangster International Airport)
  • Hip Strip entertainment district
  • World-class golf courses
  • Widest selection of all-inclusive properties
  • Shopping, nightlife, and cultural attractions

Negril

Famous for its 7-mile white sand beach and laid-back vibe. Perfect for:

  • Stunning sunsets from cliff-side resorts
  • Adults-only romantic properties
  • Watersports and snorkeling
  • Rick's Café cliff jumping
  • Relaxed, bohemian atmosphere

Ocho Rios

Lush, tropical paradise on Jamaica's north coast known for:

  • Dunn's River Falls climbing adventure
  • Zip-lining through rainforest canopies
  • Family-friendly resort options
  • Dolphin encounters and water parks
  • Quieter, more intimate setting

South Coast (Whitehouse)

Off-the-beaten-path luxury with fewer crowds and authentic Jamaican experiences.

Best All-Inclusive Resort Brands in Jamaica

Sandals Resorts (Adults-Only Luxury)

Jamaica is home to 7 Sandals properties - more than any other destination:

  • Sandals Montego Bay - Original Sandals property, swim-up bars, lively atmosphere
  • Sandals Royal Caribbean - Private offshore island, British manor charm
  • Sandals Ochi - Largest Sandals resort, 16 restaurants, extensive amenities
  • Sandals Dunn's River - Newest property, ultra-modern, near Dunn's River Falls
  • Sandals Negril - Seven Mile Beach location, romantic atmosphere
  • Sandals South Coast - Intimate, off-the-beaten-path luxury
  • Sandals Royal Plantation - Butler-included luxury, villa-style accommodations

All Sandals include: Unlimited premium spirits, gourmet dining, water sports, scuba diving, airport transfers, tips, and more.

Beaches Resorts (Family All-Inclusive)

Sandals' family-friendly sister brand with supervised kids programs:

  • Beaches Negril - Water park, kids clubs, family suites

Best Time to Visit Jamaica

  • Peak Season (December-April): Perfect weather, 80s daily, minimal rain. Higher prices but guaranteed sunshine.
  • Shoulder Season (May-June, November): Great weather, fewer crowds, better resort deals
  • Summer (July-August): Hot and humid but significant savings. Brief afternoon showers.
  • Fall (September-October): Best deals, hurricane season (though Jamaica is rarely hit)

Getting to Jamaica

Montego Bay (MBJ) - Sangster International Airport:

  • Direct flights from most major US cities (2-4 hours)
  • Serves Montego Bay and Negril resorts (15-90 min transfers)

Kingston (KIN) - Norman Manley International Airport:

  • Capital city airport, longer transfers to resort areas
  • Used less frequently for leisure travel
